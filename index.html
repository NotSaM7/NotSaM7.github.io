How it went

Case Studies

Quant Trading App

A dashboard that trades an NSE universe in parallel.

Problem

Watching NSE stocks against SMA, RSI, and ATR signals is too much to do by hand — and acting late on a signal defeats the whole point of a strategy.

Approach

Built a parallel strategy engine that scores every ticker across multiple indicators, with automated stop-loss, one-click profit booking, and 6–12 month backtesting — wrapped in a JWT-secured portfolio API.

Outcome

A live dashboard where the same engine that runs signals also backtests them, deployed to Vercel — a rule book of indicator logic turned into a tool anyone can run.

Stock Trading Simulation Game

Learning the market without risking a rupee.

Problem

Practising on the Indian market means risking real money — and most paper-trading tools are clunky or built around US tickers.

Approach

Pulled live NSE data through FastAPI and yfinance, executed virtual orders at real prices with zero financial risk, and tracked portfolio P&L on interactive line and candlestick charts.

Outcome

A realistic simulator for practising order timing and reading charts before ever touching a real position — same market, no downside.
